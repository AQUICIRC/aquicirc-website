The University of Padova, Italy, announced a 24-month postdoctoral research position within the AQUICIRC project, focusing on groundwater flow, contaminant transport, and sustainable water management.

The research involves developing and validating numerical groundwater flow and reactive transport models, investigating contaminant fate in Managed Aquifer Recharge (MAR) systems, and evaluating strategies to improve groundwater quality.

The position offers opportunities to contribute to international research on MAR and circular water management.

**Application deadline:** 10 November 2026 (closed).
