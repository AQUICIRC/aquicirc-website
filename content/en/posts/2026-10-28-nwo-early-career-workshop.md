The NWO Early Career Workshop in Earth and Environmental Sciences will take place on 8 December 2026, from 13:00 to 17:30, at Mariënhof in Amersfoort, the Netherlands.

The workshop offers early-career researchers an opportunity to connect with peers, exchange ideas, and develop their research careers within the Earth and Environmental Sciences community.

Registration is required.
