AQUICIRC researchers Reynold Chow and Victor Bense from Wageningen University & Research visited the Institut National Agronomique de Tunisie (INAT), hosted by Mohamed Fethi Ben Hamouda, to conduct hydrogeophysical investigations along the Wadi of Kurait in Tunisia.

Using the TEM2Go system, the team carried out geophysical surveys to characterize subsurface geology and assess existing and potential sites for Managed Aquifer Recharge (MAR).

The field investigations were accompanied by the AQUICIRC Tunisian kick-off meeting, bringing together researchers and local stakeholders to discuss sustainable groundwater management and opportunities for collaboration.

During the meeting, Victor Bense presented preliminary survey findings, while Reynold Chow introduced the AQUICIRC project's objectives, partner roles, and opportunities for collaboration.

Eve-An van de Brug from Wageningen University & Research also engaged local stakeholders through a water management survey.

The activities highlighted the importance of combining hydrogeophysical investigations, stakeholder engagement, and international collaboration to support sustainable water management and MAR development.

Special thanks to Mohamed Fethi Ben Hamouda, the INAT team, and all participating stakeholders for their hospitality and valuable contributions.
