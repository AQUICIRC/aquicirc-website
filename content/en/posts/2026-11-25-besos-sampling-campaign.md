On 28 September 2026, the AQUICIRC team from the Polytechnic University of Catalonia (UPC) conducted a sampling campaign at the Besòs river basin study site in Spain.

The campaign forms part of AQUICIRC's research activities on managed aquifer recharge (MAR) for circular water management.
